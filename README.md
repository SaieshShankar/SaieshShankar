# Saiesh Shankar

### Machine Learning · Artificial Intelligence · AI Alignment & Safety

I'm a computer science undergraduate at PES University, developing my skills through ML projects and exploratory work on LLM behaviour. My interests centre on **AI alignment and safety**, particularly **AI control, monitoring, evaluation, and multi-agent behaviour**.

## Selected projects

| Project | Focus | What to explore |

Each repository documents its own scope and provenance. Pilot results are preliminary; projects based on existing frameworks retain their upstream attribution.
